Turn RFQ emails into quotes

  • Reads the request in the email body and the specs in attached PDFs
  • Fills a standard order form with grade, dimensions, quantity and certs
  • Checks live inventory and drafts the quote reply

AI you can check

AI only reads the request. Plain code fills and checks the order form, every value shows the line of the email or PDF it came from, and nothing is sent until someone on your team approves the reply.

Connected to the rest of RudCo

  • Approved quotes become orders in Automated Click-to-Buy
  • The same order carries on into Logistics Visibility and Compliance & Traceability
  • No re-keying between the inbox, the quote and the purchase order

Procurement Planning Tools

Plan ahead instead of reacting

Give buyers the visibility and controls they need to stay in front of demand — forecasting future needs, catching shortages early, and keeping every shipment fully documented and traceable.

Projected-need dashboards

Forward-looking views of upcoming demand by material, grade, and site so buyers can line up supply before it's urgent.

Shortage alerts

Proactive notifications when inventory, lead times, or supplier coverage put an upcoming need at risk — with recommended actions.

COAs, MTRs, mill certs, and country-of-origin records are captured with every shipment and tied back to the PO, lot, and heat number. Full chain-of-custody traceability from source to delivery, with audit-ready exports for ISO, AS9100, ITAR, and customer quality reviews.
